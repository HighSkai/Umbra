Thermostat

Matter, coming next

Thermostat support is planned through Matter, which reads the thermostat locally without the maker's cloud. Pairing will need the Matter code printed on the thermostat (often a QR code labelled for smart-home setup) and has to happen on the same network. What you'll see depends on what the thermostat exposes over Matter: usually temperature, setpoints and mode.
